## About Me

I am **Trần Việt Dũng**, an undergraduate **Computer Science** student at **Ho Chi Minh City University of Technology – Vietnam National University (HCMUT-VNU)**.

My academic and research orientation lies at the intersection of **Quantum Computing** and **High-Performance Quantum Computing (HPQC)**. I am particularly interested in how classical systems engineering, parallel architectures, and algorithmic optimization can push the boundaries of quantum circuit simulation and bridge theoretical quantum algorithms with scalable computation.

- **Affiliation:** Department of Computer Science, HCMUT-VNU *(Class of `2025`)*
- **Core Focus:** Quantum Computing · High-Performance Quantum Computing (HPQC)
- **Academic Goal:** Seeking undergraduate research opportunities, lab collaborations, and graduate fellowships in **Quantum Computing & HPQC**
